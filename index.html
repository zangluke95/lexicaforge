How to Use LexicaForge

LexicaForge is a free, browser-based English word tool that helps you solve word puzzles, improve your vocabulary, and dominate word games. No account, no download, no cost — just enter your letters and go.

Word Unscrambler

Type any combination of letters (up to 15) into the Unscramble tab and click the button. LexicaForge will find every valid English word that can be formed using those letters. Results are sorted by length and marked with Scrabble point values. Click any word to instantly see its definition.

Anagram Solver

An anagram uses exactly the same letters as the original word, rearranged. For example, LISTEN and SILENT are anagrams. The Anagram tab offers two modes: "full anagrams" (uses all letters exactly once) and "partial anagrams" (allows using a subset of letters).

Word Pattern Finder

The Word Finder tab is ideal for crossword and Wordle players. Use ? for a single unknown letter and * for any string of letters. For example, entering TH?NK will find THINK and THANK, while S*ING will find SING, SWING, STING, SPRING, and more.

English Dictionary

Look up any English word to get its definition, phonetic pronunciation, part of speech, and example usage sentences — all powered by the Free Dictionary API with no API key required.

Scrabble Point Values

  • 1 point: A, E, I, O, U, L, N, S, T, R
  • 2 points: D, G
  • 3 points: B, C, M, P
  • 4 points: F, H, V, W, Y
  • 5 points: K
  • 8 points: J, X
  • 10 points: Q, Z
